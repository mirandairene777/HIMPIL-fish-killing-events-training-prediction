**Subject: Request for fish-kill reports, investigation records, and response records for Laguna de Bay (2016–2025)**

Dear [Recipient name],

We are [Researcher name], [position] at [Institution], working under the supervision of [Adviser name] on the student research project **HIMPIL: A Machine Learning-Based Early Warning and Advisory System for Fish Kill Events in Laguna de Bay**. The project is currently a feasibility assessment. No model, risk score, or warning service is deployed.

We request event-level records for **1 January 2016 to 31 December 2025**, or the longest available portion of that period, concerning fish kills and related fish-health incidents in Laguna de Bay:

- **`BFAR-INC-01` — Fish-kill reports and investigation records.** Incident identifier, date and time, municipality and barangay, waterbody and lake zone, coordinates if held, species affected, estimated mortality and unit, affected area, reported cause, response action, laboratory or diagnostic result, investigation status, source, and report date.
- **Response and assessment records.** Copies or indexes of BFAR response reports, damage or loss assessments, laboratory results, and after-action notes that can be released.
- **Reporting-completeness information.** A statement of the period covered by the records and whether a search found no responsive record. If your office has no record for a requested period, a written statement to that effect is valuable. Please do not treat an absence of records as proof that no fish kill occurred unless your records support that conclusion.
- **A document index**, if the underlying reports cannot be released in full, so that we can identify what exists and request a redacted version through the proper process.

Please provide event-level records rather than only aggregate counts. If a report contains personal information, you may redact names, signatures, and contact details that are not necessary for the research. If the request belongs with another office, please tell us where to route it. If a data-sharing agreement, official request form, fee, or FOI request is required, please advise us.

We will keep the original files unchanged, record source hashes and permission conditions, use the data only for this research, and not redistribute restricted files. We will not use during-event or post-event measurements as predictors, and we will not expand an aggregate statement into individual incidents. Findings will be reported at the level your terms permit, with attribution.

Please contact [Researcher name] at [Email address] or [Phone number] if anything needs clarification.
