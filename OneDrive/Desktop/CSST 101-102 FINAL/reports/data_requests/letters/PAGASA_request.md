We are [Researcher name], [position] at [Institution], working under the supervision of [Adviser name] on the student research project **HIMPIL: A Machine Learning-Based Early Warning and Advisory System for Fish Kill Events in Laguna de Bay**. The project is currently a feasibility assessment. No model, risk score, or warning service is deployed.

We request the following research data for **1 January 2016 to 31 December 2025**, or the longest available portion of that period:

- **`PAGASA-WX-01` — Daily weather observations.** Date, station identifier and name, latitude and longitude if available, daily rainfall (mm), air temperature (°C), wind speed with unit, wind direction, QA flag, completeness flag, and source or station metadata.
- Station metadata needed to understand the records: station name, station ID, coordinates, elevation if held, active period, and any change in instrumentation or location.
- A data dictionary or note describing units, time zone, missing-value codes, and quality-control conventions.

We are requesting research data, not a certified weather certificate. Daily values are preferred; if only a coarser resolution is available, please state the available resolution and period. Please preserve missing station-days as blanks or documented missing codes rather than interpolating them, and include completeness flags where they exist.

We are using the published student/academic research route on PAGASA's Climate Data page and will submit the **Researcher Climatological Raw Data Request Form (CADS-02A)** and the requested supporting documents. We understand that the current published requirements include a school ID or registration form, a copy of the approved research proposal, the Terms and Conditions of Use for Climatological Data, and a formal request letter addressed to the PAGASA Administrator and noted by the research adviser or a committee member. We will comply with the Terms and Conditions of Use.

If this request should be routed to another office, or if additional forms, fees, or a data-sharing agreement are required, please advise us. If some variables are unavailable, please indicate which fields can be released and at what resolution.

The data will be used only for this student research. We will keep the original files unchanged, record source and permission details, and not redistribute restricted files. We will not use during-event or post-event measurements as predictors. Findings will be reported with attribution and at the level your terms permit.
