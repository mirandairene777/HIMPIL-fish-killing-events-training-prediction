**Subject: Request for Laguna de Bay water-quality, station, incident, and reporting-completeness data (2016–2025)**

Dear [Recipient name],

We are [Researcher name], [position] at [Institution], working under the supervision of [Adviser name] on the student research project **HIMPIL: A Machine Learning-Based Early Warning and Advisory System for Fish Kill Events in Laguna de Bay**. The project is currently a feasibility assessment. No model, risk score, or warning service is deployed.

We request the following non-confidential data for the period **1 January 2016 to 31 December 2025**, or the longest available portion of that period. Earlier digital records are welcome if they are already available.

- **`LLDA-WQ-01` — Dated lake and tributary water-quality observations.** Daily or finest available resolution: observation date/timestamp, station identifier and name, coordinates if held, waterbody and lake zone, dissolved oxygen (mg/L), water temperature (°C), pH, nitrate, phosphate, ammonia if routinely measured, BOD, chlorophyll-a if routinely measured, units, analytical method, QA flag, and source record identifier.
- **`LLDA-ST-01` — Station registry and coordinates.** Station ID, station name, latitude, longitude, coordinate precision, geodetic datum, waterbody, lake zone, station type, active dates, and notes.
- **`LLDA-QA-01` — Annual aggregation and QA/QC rules.** The rules used to produce the published annual station summaries, including inclusion/exclusion decisions, detection limits, missing-value codes, units, and QA-flag definitions.
- **`LLDA-INC-01` — Fish-kill incident and investigation records.** Event-level records with dates, locations, species, estimated mortality and unit, affected area, reported cause, investigation status, source, and report date. Please do not send only an aggregate count if event-level records exist.
- **`LLDA-COMP-01` — Reporting-completeness records.** Period- and area-level evidence of expected reports, reports received, and any confirmed zero-report periods, so that the absence of a report is not mistaken for a confirmed non-event.
- **`LLDA-ZONE-01` — Lake-zone boundary and station crosswalk.** An authoritative zone boundary or a source-approved station-to-zone lookup table. Please state the effective date and source.

The attached request matrix gives the exact field list, preferred format, and acceptance criteria for each request. We can receive CSV, XLSX, PDF, or GIS files, provided the fields, units, date meanings, and station identifiers are documented. Missing values should be left blank or given a documented missing code; please do not substitute zero. If daily data are unavailable, please state the finest resolution that exists.

If any requested material is restricted, confidential, or held by another office, please tell us the restriction and whether a redacted summary or aggregate can be released. If a data-sharing agreement, official request form, fee, or different recipient is required, please advise us before processing the request.

We will keep the original files unchanged, record source hashes and permission conditions, use the data only for this research, and not redistribute restricted files. We will not use during-event or post-event measurements as predictors, and we will not treat an absent news report as a confirmed negative. Findings will be reported at the level your terms permit, with attribution.

Please contact [Researcher name] at [Email address] or [Phone number] if anything needs clarification. We would be grateful for a response at your earliest convenience.
