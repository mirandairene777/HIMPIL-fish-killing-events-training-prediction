Dear Mayor [Mayor name],

We are [Researcher name], [position] at [Institution], working under the supervision of [Adviser name] on the student research project **HIMPIL: A Machine Learning-Based Early Warning and Advisory System for Fish Kill Events in Laguna de Bay**. The project is currently a feasibility assessment. No model, risk score, or warning service is deployed.

We request non-confidential records for **1 January 2016 to 31 December 2025**, or the longest available portion of that period, concerning fish kills and fish-mortality reports in [City/Municipality]:

- **`LGU-INC-01` — Fish-kill and response records.** Date and time, barangay, waterbody, species affected, estimated mortality and unit, action taken, damage or loss assessment, report source, and any available investigation or laboratory result.
- **Reporting-completeness information.** The period covered by the available records, the office that receives fish-kill reports, and whether a search found no responsive record. A written statement that no record exists for a defined period is useful, provided the period covered by the search is stated.
- **Any public advisories, response logs, or water-quality observations** that the LGU holds and can release, with the station or sampling location identified.
- **A document index**, if the underlying records cannot be released in full, so that we can identify what exists and request a redacted version through the proper process.

Please provide event-level records rather than only an aggregate count. If a record contains personal information, you may redact names, signatures, and contact details that are not necessary for the research. Please do not provide an estimated loss or mortality figure without labeling it as an estimate and stating its basis.

If this request belongs with another office, please route it or tell us where to send it. If a formal request form, fee, data-sharing agreement, or FOI request is required, please advise us.

We will keep the original files unchanged, record source hashes and permission conditions, use the data only for this research, and not redistribute restricted files. We will not use during-event or post-event measurements as predictors, and we will not treat the absence of a local record as proof that no fish kill occurred unless reporting completeness is documented. Findings will be reported at the level your terms permit, with attribution.

Please contact [Researcher name] at [Email address] or [Phone number] if anything needs clarification.
